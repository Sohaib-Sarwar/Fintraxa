## Patterns we adopt

- **HeroFigure.** One figure per screen, set large in Inter Tight tabular figures, with a muted label above it. Show "as of" under market totals. The figure answers the screen's single question: Today = net worth or spendable, Invest = portfolio value (refs 2, 6, 7).
- **DeltaPill.** Placed directly under the hero and above the chart. Shows the change in Rs and % plus the period ("today", "1M"). This is the only place on the screen that uses green or red, with text plus a ▲/▼ glyph so it is not colour-only. The change appears before any breakdown of totals (refs 4, 7).
- **StatRow.** At most three secondary figures below the chart, e.g. today's return · total return · invested (ref 7). There are no cards behind them, only hairline dividers.
- **NeedsYouStrip.** A thin strip on Today for items due soon: upcoming bills, recurring charges, uncategorised transactions to review (ref 6, "Upcoming"). It disappears when nothing is due, and promotions never go in it (ref 5).
- **ListRow (holding).** Columns: name/ticker and units on the left, value and day change on the right, with an optional tiny sparkline. The list is plain, full-width and dense, sortable by value, and shown straight away. No per-row cards and no search gate (refs 1, 7). Swipe actions are an optional extra later (ref 2).
- **ListRow (transaction).** Columns: category glyph, merchant/note and date on the left, amount in tabular figures on the right. Rows are grouped by day under sticky date headers. Tapping a row opens a BottomSheet, not a new screen.
- **BottomSheet.** Used for detail, confirm and add. The key amount leads, followed by one grouped summary block and a close button top-right. The primary action is pinned at the thumb zone and disabled until the input is valid (refs 5, 8). Swipe-up or hold-to-confirm is reserved for irreversible actions (ref 2).
- **SegmentedControl.** Chart timeframe chips (1D·1W·1M·1Y·MAX, ref 4/7) and the Expense / Income / Transfer switch on the add sheet. Selected state = ink fill. Gold is not used here.
- **AmountKeypad.** A large live amount sits at the top, category chips in the middle and the keypad at the bottom, with a utility row (date · account · note) above the keypad. The last-used category is pre-selected (refs 10, 11).
- **CategoryGrid.** 4 columns of monochrome glyphs with labels, recents first. Gold marks only the selected item.
- **AreaChart / Sparkline.** A single line with a faint fill, no gridlines, and a min/max label or scrub tooltip so values stay readable (ref 1). The line follows the period's direction (green or red), and scrubbing replaces the hero value with the scrubbed point.
- **EmptyState.** One sentence and one action, e.g. "No spending yet. Add your first expense" plus the + button (ref 11). No illustrations heavier than a glyph.
- **Tab bar with centre +.** Today · Money · (+) · Invest · Me. The + is a 56 px ink/paper circle raised above the bar, respecting the safe-area inset, with press feedback and the label "Add". It opens the AmountKeypad sheet and is hidden on Me and settings screens (ref 12). Unlike Wise's abandoned centred "Send" tab (ref 5), it is an action and not a destination, so it never becomes a selected tab.
- **Sign-in and biometric unlock.** The unlock screen shows only the wordmark, the user's name and a "Unlock" button that triggers the system biometric prompt automatically on open. "Use PIN / password" stays visible underneath (ref 13). Biometrics are offered once, after the first sign-in, with a one-line privacy note. We never draw a fake fingerprint or face UI. After 1–2 failures the PIN shows. Re-check before revealing masked figures or changing security settings.

## Patterns we avoid

- **Gradients and glows.** No gradient cards, no neon or green glows, no glassmorphism (ref 9). Surfaces are flat ink or paper with hairline borders.
- **Coloured or "bubble" card backgrounds.** Tinted cards and padded bubbles waste space and bury content (refs 1, 9). Grouping comes from spacing and dividers.
- **Green/red as decoration.** Green is not used for CTAs, brand moments or "bullish" badges. Colour means profit or loss only, and gold is the single accent (ref 9).
- **Cluttered dashboards.** No widget walls, promo tiles or "invite and earn" above the primary task (ref 5). The hero and up to three stats are the budget. Anything more goes one tap deeper (ref 3).
- **Hiding the primary list.** Holdings and transactions are never behind a search box, pull-up drawer or extra tap (ref 1).
- **Unlabelled charts.** No chart without a value anchor or a scrub readout, and no candlesticks on overview screens (refs 1, 9).
- **Gamified feedback.** No confetti, streaks or celebratory motion on trades. Motion stays short and functional.
- **Overriding the user's theme.** Follow the system light/dark setting and the user's choice. Never force one (ref 1).
- **Custom biometric UI or weak hidden fallbacks.** Use the OS prompt and keep the fallback visible (ref 13).
